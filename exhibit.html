an exhibit in eight rooms · n = 1

mostly the machine

i logged every song i played for seven years and assumed the log was a record of me. four fifths of it was a machine advancing a queue.

eight rooms of finding that out: what i measured, what the measuring broke, what i had to bury. every dot behind this text is a play, all one colour because nobody has asked yet who pressed play. from the next room on, mint is a play i chose and violet is one the machine served. all the music here is mine. sound on if you can.

the sky

the universe

every artist i played at least 50 times is a star. where a star sits means nothing on its own: the linked ones come from a seeded layout of what i played back to back, and the rest ring the edge in order of plays. the other artists are dust. drag to turn it, tap a star.

19 in every 100 plays i tapped, 17 arrived shuffled, 64 the queue served (a few of those started some other way). in this view a tap means the strict set (play button, click, remote): 11.5 in every 100.

a game of links

the chain

make a chain: start anywhere and follow a real link. 482 links join 293 of the 388 stars of my sky; on 264 of them i made the jump by hand at least 3 times.

mint: a link i crossed by hand. violet: the queue took it, the next song starting on its own with shuffle off. both colours: both did. every hop is a note, and your chain plays back as a riff you can send.

the rain

first meetings

6,277 artists in the order they first played for me, each falling into the pool of who played it first: my hand, shuffle, or the queue. about two in three, the queue played them first.

mint: my hand. amber: shuffle. violet: the queue, the next song starting on its own with shuffle off, which includes my own albums and playlists running on. type an artist to find it, or guess who played it first. the log only begins partway into my listening, so the first stretch of the rain includes artists i already knew; artists i only heard for under 30 seconds are not here.

a patience game

on loop

hold to loop. 12,390 times my log shows the same song twice in a row. most of those repeats sit in streaks of 14 or more.

the tower: a floor for each streak length, and a floor's area is the repeats it holds. while you hold, one bar keeps looping and every loop climbs a floor. numbers only: no titles, by design.

hand and machine

who held the wheel

5,489 listening sessions. inside them the wheel changed hands 20,688 times (bundled reading). mint = runs i started by hand, violet = runs i didn't start by hand.

a new session starts when more than 30 minutes separate the ends of two counted plays (30 s or more). a run = plays in a row with the same driver. bundled: by hand = track row, play button, remote, skip forward or back. strict: only the first three, so a skip counts as not-by-hand. everything else = not by hand, including shuffle and my own albums and playlists running on.
