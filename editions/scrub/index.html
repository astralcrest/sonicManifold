how

drag a finger or the mouse across the dim map, or focus it and use the arrow keys. the artist you rest on plays and stays lit.

press replay my path to hear your last twelve stops in order, or next unheard artist to hop to the closest one.

colors are genre families, size is how much i played an artist. the big number counts artists heard.

the fine print

clips are 30 second previews from Apple, only where one exists. where none exists the artist lights silent and the tag says no clip.

a clip starts once you rest on an artist for a beat, so a fast sweep lights without chopping. your path is not saved; only the sound on/off choice is remembered on this device.

38 of 100: of each artist's six nearest in sound, the share in its own genre family. a random pair shares one 17 of 100 times.
