how

play A and B (both buttons or the cards), then pick the one you like more. no names, and no pick until you have heard both.

round 2: two new clips, each the nearest in sound to one of the first. half the rounds show names; the map lights up and you can tap a lit shape to hear it.

count the switches: more switching with names shown means the names move your pick. works with a keyboard too: a and b play, no mouse needed.
