how

drag either spot and the artist nearest halfway rings up and plays.

walk the route plays the chain of nearest neighbors from your first spot, through the halfway artist, to your second. next stop skips ahead. keys: r picks two spots, n is next stop.

read the big number as the most hops from the halfway artist to either of your spots. colors are sound families; bigger shapes are played more.

the fine print

a toy on this screen, not my data. the halfway point is the plain midpoint of your two spots on my sound map; the artist is the nearest one with a clip.

steps are counted in the neighbor graph: each artist joined to its six nearest in sound, which can sit far apart on this flat map. so halfway on the map is rarely halfway in sound: in 2,000 pairs from pick two spots for me, the halfway artist sat on a shortest route between them about 3 times in 100.

clips are 30 s public previews and only play with sound on. your spots are not saved; only the sound on/off choice is remembered on this device.
