sound comes first: turn the sound chip on, then place a spot. on a laptop, click and drag. no mouse needed: use pick two spots for me. keys: r picks two spots, n is next stop.

how

turn sound on, tap two spots on the map, then drag either one. the artist nearest halfway rings up and plays.

tap walk the route, then next stop to skip ahead. the route is the chain of nearest neighbors from your first spot, through the halfway artist, to your second.

read the big number as the most hops from the halfway artist to either of your spots. colors are sound families; bigger shapes are played more.

the fine print

a toy on this screen, not my data. the halfway point is the plain midpoint of your two spots on my sound map; the artist is the nearest one with a clip.

steps are counted in the neighbor graph: each artist joined to its six nearest on the map, from the meeting artist to the artist nearest each spot.

clips are 30 s public previews and only play with sound on. nothing you touch is stored.
