how

press play: the evening is heard song by song to the end, with the next song named. on a laptop, the arrow keys step and s swaps.

use next song, back one song, or tap a dot to jump. the bright line is the night so far; a colored ring marks a new family.

press swap: the next song is replaced with a near one, and the rose line bends away from the real purple one. bigger number, farther bend.

the fine print

from my log: auto-play's own streak of songs stays close in sound. when i broke the streak the next song sat farther away, and when shuffle broke it, farther still. a direction, not a size.

bend = average distance on the map, as a share of its width. yours: song by song against the evening as it happened. mine: my own presses against auto-play's most common next song. a toy, not a test.
