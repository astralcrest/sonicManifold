how

on a laptop hold the left or right arrow. on the map, drag a handle inward: the farther, the harder it pulls. tap a target to jump to it.

each stop plays seven seconds. hear the path replays every stop in order; press n or tap a numbered pebble to skip ahead.

read the path: ice where sound won, mint where my plays won. pebbles take their family color; a ring marks a new family.

the fine print

a toy rule on this screen, not my data. the dot pulls toward the nearest artist in sound; the star pulls toward the most played artist within reach.

the share is how far each handle moved the point. it is not a measurement of me.
