open question:who should draw the line?

turn sound on with the button at the top, then draw. on a laptop, drag with the mouse; press n for the next stop.

how

drag across the map and let go. the curve turns into numbered stops, about one per 90 px.

each stop is the nearest artist with a clip, within 48 px of the curve. the stops play in the order you drew them.

draw fast and the stops lean to the big names near the curve. draw slow and you get the exact nearest. tap a number to jump there.

the fine print

the map places artists by how their music sounds. color is the genre family. size is how often i played them.

a clip is 30 s of a public preview of that artist, not necessarily a song i played. artists with no clip are skipped, and an artist is never used twice on one radio.

fast means a stroke faster than about 0.6 px per millisecond.
