what the body did not do

each lane was pre-declared on a hash before any number: unit, clock, arms, model, kill rule, and the smallest effect it could detect at 80 percent power (the blind spot). intervals come from a 28-day block bootstrap; nulls are tested against within-day permutations. a lane is "killed" when a control removes it, "reversed" when the sign came out against the pre-declared direction, "null" when the interval holds zero with the blind spot stated, "descriptive" when it is a number with caveats and no claim. nothing on this page is a claim that music changes the body.
