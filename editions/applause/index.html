the fine print

nothing is recorded; only a loudness number is read, never audio. the mic is read about 60 times a second as one number and the sound is thrown away at once.

a loud room will skip itself. the threshold is high, a skip needs a sharp jump over the room's usual level, and there is a 3 s cooldown. test it in the room.
