open question:whose hand skips when the room is the hand?

sound: the song plays only when sound is on. claps work either way.

how

tap listen, allow the mic. a loud clap plays the next song.

quiet keeps the song. after a skip it waits 3 seconds.

no mic? use tap to clap. the counts stay on this tablet.

the fine print

nothing is recorded; only a loudness number is read, never audio. the mic is read about 60 times a second as one number and the sound is thrown away at once.

a loud room will skip itself. the threshold is high, a skip needs a sharp jump over the room's usual level, and there is a 3 s cooldown. test it in the room.

the songs are a toy auto-play walk through my map. the two counts are saved on this tablet only, under tonight's date, and nowhere else.
