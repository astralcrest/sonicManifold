family crossings: steps where the next play is a different family

press play the sitting. the ribbon lights up one play at a time.

    takeaway:one sitting is a path, not a point. in these 40 picked sittings, just over half the steps change family.

    open question:does a sitting that crosses more families feel more like searching?

    how

    press play the sitting and turn sound on at the top. each play gets a few seconds of clip, then it moves on by itself.

    in a hurry: tap a cell, a shape on the map, or next stop. keys: space plays or stops, arrows step, n is next sitting.

    read it: ribbon color = family. the marks follow the key above: my press, auto-play, shuffle. a tick above a cell = a crossing.

    the fine print

    each sitting is a real stretch of plays from my log, in order. a crossing is a step where the artist's family differs from the last artist's. the family is the one the sound map gives that artist, not the song.

    the 40 sittings were picked to have 12 to 30 plays, at least 8 artists, mostly auto-play; not a random sample.

    a clip is a short piece of a public preview of that artist, not always the song that played; the line under the buttons names both. when the same artist comes twice in a row, the next clip starts a few seconds later so it does not repeat.
