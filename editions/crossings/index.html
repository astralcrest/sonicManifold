how many genres does one night cross?

loading the night

family crossings: steps where the next play is a different family

press play the night. the ribbon lights up one play at a time.

    takeaway:one night is a path, not a point. most steps stay in a family, a few jump.

    open question:does a night that crosses more families feel more like searching?

    how

    press play the night and turn sound on at the top. each play gets a few seconds of clip, then it moves on by itself.

    in a hurry: tap a cell, a shape on the map, or next stop. keys: space plays or stops, arrows step, n is next evening.

    read it: ribbon color = family. star = my press, ring = auto-play, diamond = shuffle. a tick above a cell = a crossing.

    the fine print

    each evening is a real stretch of plays from my log, in order. a crossing is a step where the artist's family differs from the last artist's. the family is the one the sound map gives that artist, not the song.

    a clip is a short piece of a public preview of that artist, not always the song that played; the line under the buttons names the real one. when the same artist comes twice in a row, the next clip starts a few seconds later so it does not repeat.
