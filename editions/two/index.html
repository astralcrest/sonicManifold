      short lists place poorly. tap a shape to name the artist; tap a stop on a path to hear from there.

      the fine print

      auto-play’s rule walks 40 steps twice. mine uses the settings matched to my own listening, started from your top artist. yours is matched to your list: an export uses the artists you played back to back; a pasted list uses neighbors in your ranking. the habit dial scales how hard each rule repeats a pair in both. one walk each, so another seed can differ.

      no compatibility score: these are places on one listener's map, not a verdict on two people.
