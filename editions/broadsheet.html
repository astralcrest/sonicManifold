by astralcrest, listening correspondentcounted from the start of every play

2019 to 2026. Every play begins somewhere. Some begin with my hand: a track row, the play button, a skip forward or back. The rest begin because the queue moved on without asking me. Sorted that way, 81 of every 100 plays were chosen for me, not by me.the strictest count asks for more: a play counts as mine only when i pressed the play button, a track row or a remote. My share falls from 19 in 100 to 11.5.

Shuffle accounts for 17 of every 100. The app’s own queue served 64, the largest share, and the quietest: nothing announces it, the next song simply arrives.

The strictest count is harsher. Keep only starts that leave no doubt and my share falls from 19 in 100 to 11.5. The app changed what it logs in october 2023, and the strict count is the one that holds across that change.

What i chose and what i was handed are different music. Ambient and lofi lean on the machine; hip-hop and r&b are the ones i go and get. Both stories continue inside.
