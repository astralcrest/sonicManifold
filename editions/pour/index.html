how

slide the line, or tilt the phone, to set auto-play's share. on a laptop: the slider, the left and right arrow keys, and n for the next step.

bar color = who picked the step: mint my press, violet auto-play, amber shuffle. the small dot beside each bar is the sound family; a bigger dot marks a change. pebble color is the family; a bigger pebble, more plays.

the fine print

a toy rule on this screen. a press of mine is a jump to one of my 60 most played artists, weighted by plays; a shuffle pick is any of those 60, evenly; an auto-play pick is the nearest artist in sound that the path has not just visited.

64 of 100 is the setting that matches my log: the other 36 split 19 my press, 17 shuffle, and the slider keeps that ratio at every setting. any other setting is a toy, not a measurement of you.

nothing is stored. the tilt reading stays on the phone and is never sent anywhere.
