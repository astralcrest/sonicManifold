how

press hear. the clips play in step order and keep going until you stop. next step skips ahead; so does a tap on any pebble or on the column.

slide the line, or tilt the phone, to set auto-play's share. on a laptop use the slider, the left and right arrow keys, and n for next step.

wide mint bar and star = my press. narrow violet bar and ring = auto-play. the small dot beside each bar is the sound family; a bigger dot marks a change.

the fine print

a toy rule on this screen. a pick of mine is a jump to one of my 60 most played artists; an auto-play pick is the nearest artist in sound that the path has not just visited.

64 of 100 is the setting that matches my log. yours is a toy setting, not a measurement of you.

nothing is stored. the tilt reading stays on the phone and is never sent anywhere.
