the map

artists that shared my evenings sit near each other. far apart means little. the map learned from every play, and the app started most of them. the map is the picture, not the proof.

a personal word2vec on 5,489 sittings (a 30-minute silence closes one), then umap to three dimensions, cosine, 18 neighbors. 1,986 artists placed; the rest wait at the edge. the spread around each star is drawing, not data.

the embedding is trained on all 97,427 plays, so its geometry reflects the queue as much as me. that is why no finding on this page is read off the map; the refereed numbers below come from genre tags, not from distances.
