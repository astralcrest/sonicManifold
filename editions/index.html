twelve editions

the same findings about one listening history, 2019 to 2026, told twelve ways. pick one. the album starts on the first press in every one of them.

19 i tapped · 17 shuffle · 64 served · of every 100 plays. the strictest count says 11.5.

  1. 01

    the hall

    a museum wall: 1,986 artists as light, with their real names.

  2. 02

    the growing graph

    a film: the taste graph grows month by month, one node throws rays.

  3. 03

    the broadsheet

    a newspaper that covers one listener. the count is the lead story.

  4. 04

    the arcade

    an 8-bit cabinet. the finding is the high-score table.

  5. 05

    the flight recorder

    gauges, a paper tape of months, a scope that moves with the album.

  6. 06

    the sculpture

    one field of light: 26,000 particles in three colors, in the real shares.

  7. 07

    the poster wall

    twelve printed posters, one giant number and one sentence each.

  8. 08

    the chart

    an aged survey chart: artists as place names, density as contours.

  9. 09

    the crate

    a record store at night: 80 sleeves painted by who pressed play.

  10. 10

    the transit map

    14 genre lines, artists as stations, bridges as interchanges.

  11. 11

    the stories

    full-screen cards you tap through. no confetti, no rankings.

  12. 12

    the planetarium

    a dome of stars: artists as stars, families as constellations, a guided show.

  13. 13

    the sky

    1,986 artists as stars, colored by family or by who pressed play. tap one and it sings.

  14. 14

    who introduced us

    artists appear in the order we met, lit by whose hand pressed first. the ones i went looking for stayed.

  15. 15

    the body

    seven tests of my body answering the music, drawn as seven traces. the quiet ones are nulls; the rose ones died.

every edition reads the same data pack and the same definitions. i tapped = a play i started by hand. shuffle = the queue advanced with shuffle on. served = the queue advanced on its own. killed findings stay killed in every one. the record and the lab hold the full charts.
