the fine print

the twelve songs are drawn at random from my most played artists that have a clip. what you hold is a toy, not a measure of you.

in my log (my numbers, not yours): of songs i started myself (my press) 42 of 100 stayed, of auto-play songs 16 of 100 stayed.
