how

each song is a 30 s clip. you hear it before you decide.

hold the button (or the space bar) 2 seconds to keep it. let go early to skip. skip this song or tap a shape on the map to move on faster.

the line on the map is your order of twelve: bright so far, faint ahead. a lit shape is kept, a dark shape is skipped, colors are sound families, bigger shapes are played more.

the fine print

the twelve songs are drawn at random from my most played artists that have a clip. what you hold is a toy, not a measure of you.

my numbers, not yours: of songs not yet in my library, 42 of 100 that i pressed ended up in it, against 16 of 100 that auto-play chose. in-sample, awaiting replication.

clips are 30 s public previews of the artist, not always the song that played, and only play with sound on.
