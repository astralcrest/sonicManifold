take it home: the split stub

turn the sound chip on first. on a laptop, hold the space bar instead of the button.

how

turn sound on, then press play to hear a 30 s clip. you hear it before you decide.

hold the button (or the space bar) 2 seconds to keep it. let go early to skip. skip this song or tap a mark on the map to move on faster.

the line on the map is your order of twelve: bright so far, faint ahead. a lit mark is kept, a dark dot is skipped, colors are sound families, bigger shapes are played more.

the fine print

the twelve songs are drawn at random from my most played artists that have a clip. what you hold is a toy, not a measure of you.

in my log (my numbers, not yours): of songs i started myself (my press) 42 of 100 stayed, of auto-play songs 16 of 100 stayed.

clips are 30 s public previews of the artist, not always the song that played, and only play with sound on.
