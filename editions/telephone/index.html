the fine print

a toy rule on this screen, not my data. the numbered ones are the six nearest artists on my sound map, minus any already in the chain. they are not what anyone would pick next.

steps are counted in the neighbor graph: each artist joined to its six nearest. the toy rule alone always takes the nearest one it has not visited.

the chain is kept in this browser until someone starts a new chain. previews are 30 s clips and only play with sound on.
