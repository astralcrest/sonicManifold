open question:is a night of listening any different?

the last song is ringed. pick one of the six that sound closest to it.

how

each visitor sees the last song and its six nearest.

tap one and the chain grows. the next visitor starts there.

at ten hands the chain can play through, a few seconds each.

the fine print

a toy rule on this screen, not my data. the six are the nearest artists on my sound map, not what anyone would pick next.

steps are counted in the neighbor graph: each artist joined to its six nearest. the toy rule alone always takes the nearest one it has not visited.

the chain is kept in this browser until someone taps new chain. previews are 30 s clips and only play with sound on.
