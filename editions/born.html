how deep

who introduced us

unit = an artist with two plays or more. introducer = the arm of our first play: my tap (track row, play button, remote, skip forward or back), shuffle, or the queue on its own. an artist is "still with me" while its last play is within 90 days of the end of the log. survival by kaplan-meier, interval from a 28-day block bootstrap over the months we met. pre-registered on a hash before the numbers. this is selection, not treatment: a first tap is usually a search for a name i already carried, so the sentence says what i already knew, never that the queue fails.

what you are seeing

each spark is an artist, placed where it sits in the sky, appearing in the month of our first play. the color is the hand that started it. a spark goes to ash when our last play passes. the curve shows how many of each color were still with me a year later, over every artist with two plays or more.

1,985 artists with five plays or more are drawn (the test used 3,795 with two or more). months are counted from the first month of the log; no finer date is kept here. october 2023: the app changed how it records a start.
