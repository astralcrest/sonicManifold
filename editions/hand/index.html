open question:could you tell your own press from auto-play?

how

tap hear both, or press h. clip 1 plays, then clip 2. turn sound on at the top.

then guess for play 2: my press (key 1) or auto-play (key 2). the guess buttons wake up once clip 2 starts.

the answer shows on the map. tap next pair (key n). after ten pairs you can start over.

the fine print

each pair is two plays in a row from one night in my log, where play 2 started by my press or by auto-play. shuffle plays are left out. only pairs where both artists have a public clip are used.

a clip is 12 seconds of a 30 second public preview of that artist, not always the song that played; the now line names the real one. if both plays are the same artist, you hear the same preview twice.

ten pairs is a game, not a measurement.
