how

press play: both walks from the two seeds, about 9 seconds a stop, until two steps past where they meet. keep going plays the rest.

jump anywhere with the slider, next step, or a tap on a dot. the bright line is the path so far; a small triangle in a family's color marks a new family.

then type your own two seeds and press walk. a white cross marks where the walks first meet.

the fine print

a toy about the rule, not a guess about anyone. the rule is the one matched to my log on the first wall.

the 2,000 random pairs: two artists drawn from the whole map, each walked once with this rule and this neighborhood.

the rule nearly always takes its top pick, so the same two seeds walk the same way each time. two new seeds draws a fresh pair from my 150 most played.

previews are Apple's 30 seconds. nothing is stored but whether sound is on, in this browser: no account, no cookie, no saved seeds.
