how

tap shake or press d and the sound comes on. a phone shake keeps the sound chip as it is. two clips play in order: shuffle's landing, then my press. next clip skips ahead.

amber (shuffle) leaps to a random artist. mint (my press) goes to an artist i once pressed right before or after the old one. tap any shape to hear it. earlier shake steps back through your shakes.

read the big number as steps from the old artist to where shuffle landed. colors are sound families; bigger shapes are played more.

the fine print

shuffle here is a toy rule on this page: a random artist with a clip, drawn from the artists my presses touch. the press is real: an artist i pressed right before or after the old one (two presses in a row in my log, from a sample of pairs).

what it shows: on average shuffle jumps a little farther than my presses do, a direction, not a size. the numbers are steps in the neighbor graph (each artist joined to its six nearest), not neighborhoods.

clips are 30 s public previews and only play with sound on. motion is read only to detect a shake and is never stored.
