how

tap play all 100, or press space. it plays them in order until you pause. next play, the right arrow, or a tap on a shape skips ahead.

the center names the play, who began it, and its family. hover a shape on a laptop to see the artist.

read the rings: outer = who began it, inner = the family, a line between two inner dots = a family change, a tick outside the ring = a new sitting. bigger shape = more plays.

the fine print

the color is how each play began in my log: by my press or by auto-play. the counts are for this ring, not my whole log: of every 100 plays in the whole log, 19 are my press, 17 shuffle, 64 auto-play.

a clip is 1.5 s of a 30 s public preview of that artist, not the song that played (the center names the real one). the same artist in a row is the log, not the toy: an album, or auto-play staying put; the clip then skips ahead a few seconds so it does not stutter on one bar. if there is no clip, the shape stays lit for 1.5 s.
