what this means

This is the opening move of a bigger study: sonic manifold — seven years of one listener’s history, audited with ~30 methods, about half of which survived. The full version separates genre served from genre chosen, maps the listening as a network, and recovers intent from timing alone. This page runs only the first, portable cut on your data.
