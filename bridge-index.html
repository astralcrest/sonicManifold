bridge index

how much do you steer the queue?

drop your own spotify extended streaming history and get one number: how much more you cross between your listening communities when you pick the next track versus when the algorithm auto-advances. the path out of n = 1 — each person computes their own index, on their own machine.

◢◤ privacy

●
100% in your browser. nothing is uploaded, nothing is fetched, no server, no network. your export never leaves this tab — the math runs on this page and the raw files are dropped the moment you close it. works offline; you can disconnect and it still runs.

◢◤ how it works

a full multi-year export usually takes about 5–15s in this tab — parsing and the community search run off the main thread, so the page stays responsive while it works.
