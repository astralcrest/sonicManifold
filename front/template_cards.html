five scenes

each one is a page you can move through, with the music on, and a "the science" switch that goes three levels deep: the sentence, the numbers, the test.

  1. 01

    who pressed play

    every play as a mote of light, 2019 to 2026, colored by whose hand started it. turn the river into a clock.

    so what: the queue runs most of my days
  2. 02

    the sky

    1,986 artists as stars, placed by the evenings they shared. light one family, or color the sky by who pressed play. tap a star and it sings.

    so what: my taste has a shape
  3. 03

    who introduced us

    artists appear in the order we met, lit by whose hand pressed first, and go to ash when the last play passes.

    so what: the ones i went looking for stayed
  4. 04

    the body

    seven tests of my body answering the music, drawn as seven traces. the quiet ones are nulls with their blind spot; the rose ones died.

    so what: the music moves, the body does not
  5. 05

    the record

    a sculpture of every play, six rooms, the album as the soundtrack. the last word.

    so what: beauty counts too

my soundtrack

twenty-three songs i wrote for this, keyed and tempo-matched so one hands into the next. the first press anywhere on the site starts them; the dock at the bottom holds play, next and mute, and your choice carries across every page.

counts are over plays of 30 seconds or more. "every press" counts track row, play button, remote, skip forward and back as my hand; the strictest count leaves the skips out: 7.6 of the 19 were my back and skip presses.

october 2023: the app changed how it records a start. nothing on this site says music changes the body. one listener, one log: a direction, never a size.
