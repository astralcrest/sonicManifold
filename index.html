act four · what stays

i keep what i press.

of songs not yet in my library, i kept 42 of 100 that i pressed and 16 of 100 the queue chose. 84 of 100 of those likes landed on the first hearing, 47 seconds in. in-sample, awaiting replication.

ten songs, 47 seconds each. keep it or let it go.

how: the library is the account data, 3,529 liked tracks. kept = in it today. only songs that were not yet in it when they played count. the lane reruns unchanged on the next export.

deeper

artists my hand introduced are in the library 68 of 100 times, the queue's 30 (RR 2.27 [1.88, 2.78]); liked artists are still playing a year later 0.84 against 0.57. a like does not change the hand: +0.05 [−0.05, +0.19], killed; it marks a peak (+0.64 plays a day), not a turn. liked artists sit nearer the center of what i play, in sound (−0.18, permutation p < 0.0005, placebo +0.02 the other way; in-sample). 19 in 20 searches were artists i already knew. 44 of 45 unlikes came within 39 seconds of the like: thumb slips. on the first hearing of a song, i kept the ones i pressed more often than the ones the queue chose, even against artists i knew as well (in-sample, awaiting replication); when i keep a song on first hearing, i usually decide within the first minute. ledger rows 347 to 380.
