what this is

this is my whole Spotify log, 97,427 plays, laid out as one field of shapes: seven acts on the front, 25 walls you can play, a lab, a report and a story.

i started 19 of every 100 of those plays myself. shuffle started 17, and auto-play started 64. count only strict taps (the play button, a track's row, a remote) and my press drops to 11.5 of every 100.

when i pick the next track, it switches genre family a little more often than auto-play does. i call the ratio the bridge index. it reads 1.05, likely 1.03 to 1.08: a direction, not a size.

it holds under seed, era and playback platform, and fails three stricter checks: how the untagged jumps are handled moves it 1.00 to 1.13, and the loosest or 50-play definitions read 1.01. the report has all three.

how it was made

a Python pipeline turns the export into counts. one canvas draws every artist as a shape, placed by how it sounds, colored by who pressed play. the walls are small pages that each take one idea and let you play it. the music on the site is mine: 23 songs i made, never under a preview of anyone else's.

what was withdrawn

eighteen claims withdrawn. sixteen are graves you can visit. 0 of 11 new candidates survived unqualified.

the graveyard in the lab keeps each grave with the verdict that withdrew it. the report keeps every withdrawal on the record. every finding on the site has a ledger row: the claim, the script, the data, the result. the plain counts come straight from the export.

what stays private

astralcrest is a pseudonym. no device, place or exact timestamp leaves the export. per-play records go no finer than the day, and the hour-of-day picture is pooled over the whole log. i've held back some titles.

this page makes no requests to anyone else and runs no analytics.
