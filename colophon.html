what this is

this is my whole Spotify log, 97,427 plays, laid out as one field of dots and a handful of rooms you can walk through.

i started 19 of every 100 of those plays by hand. shuffle started 17, and the queue running on started 64. count only strict taps (the play button, a track's row, a remote) and my hand drops to 11.5 of every 100.

when i pick the next track, it switches genre family a little more often than when autoplay runs on. i call the ratio the bridge index. it reads 1.05, likely 1.03 to 1.08: a direction, not a size.

and it holds only under two conditions: how the untagged jumps are handled moves it 1.00 to 1.13; the loosest or 50-play definitions read 1.01.

how it was made

a Python pipeline turns the export into counts. a 97k-dot particle field draws them as glyphs, and each glyph's shape says who pressed play. WebAudio plays the hover notes. the music under the rooms is mine: songs i made.

the glyph renderer and the fly-to path started from what gcdatlas.com does; the rest is mine.

what was killed

the graveyard room keeps the findings i killed, each with the verdict that killed it. the technical report keeps every retraction on the record. every finding on the site has a ledger row: the claim, the script, the data, the result. the plain counts come straight from the export.

what stays private

astralcrest is a pseudonym. no device, place or exact timestamp leaves the export. per-play records go no finer than the day, and the clock room's hours are pooled over the whole log. i've held back some titles.

this page makes no requests to anyone else and runs no analytics.
