what this is

this is my whole Spotify log, 97,427 plays, laid out as one field of dots and eighteen stops you can walk through.

i started 19 of every 100 of those plays by hand. shuffle started 17, and the queue running on started 64. count only strict taps (the play button, a track's row, a remote) and my hand drops to 11.5 of every 100.

when i pick the next track, it switches genre family a little more often than when the queue runs on. i call the ratio the bridge index. it reads 1.05, likely 1.03 to 1.08: a direction, not a size.

and it holds only under two conditions: how the untagged jumps are handled moves it 1.00 to 1.13; the loosest or 50-play definitions read 1.01.

how it was made

a Python pipeline turns the export into counts. a 97k-dot particle field draws them as glyphs, and each glyph's shape says who pressed play. WebAudio plays the hover notes. the music under the stops is mine: songs i made.

the glyph renderer and the fly-to path started from what gcdatlas.com does; the rest is mine.

what was withdrawn

eighteen claims withdrawn. sixteen are graves you can visit. 0 of 11 new candidates survived unqualified.

the graveyard stop keeps each grave with the verdict that withdrew it. the report keeps every withdrawal on the record. every finding on the site has a ledger row: the claim, the script, the data, the result. the plain counts come straight from the export.

what stays private

astralcrest is a pseudonym. no device, place or exact timestamp leaves the export. per-play records go no finer than the day, and the clock stop's hours are pooled over the whole log. i've held back some titles.

this page makes no requests to anyone else and runs no analytics.
